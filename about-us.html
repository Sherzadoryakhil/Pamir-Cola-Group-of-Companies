Our mission

To make safe, good-tasting drinks, dairy and snacks that families in Afghanistan and around the world can enjoy every day.

Our vision

To be the most trusted Afghan food and beverage brand, carrying the quality of Afghanistan to every market.

Our values

Quality in every bottle, pride in local production, and respect for our customers and our people.
